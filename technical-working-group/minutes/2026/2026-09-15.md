- Being on an external server was originally a TWG decision (rather than behind an STFC firewall). This was due to the RAL IT maintenance schedule which was taking the old server offline on friday afternoons (US time).
- It would be best to finish the removal of Jenkins before this move, so we don't have to move the Jenkins instance.
- Are there any free services that are outside of STFC?
